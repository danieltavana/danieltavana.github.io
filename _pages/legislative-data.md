### Kuwait

<details>
<summary>Kuwait National Assembly Roll Call Votes Dataset (KNARCV) [1963–2016]</summary>
<p>The Kuwait National Assembly Roll Call Votes (KNARCV) Dataset includes roll call votes for each law passed by the Kuwait National Assembly (KNA) from 1963 to 2016. The dataset includes a total of 111,024 observations, i.e., roll call votes at the deputy-law level, for 3,337 unique laws.</p>
</details>
