### Kuwait

<details>
<summary>Kuwait National Assembly Roll Call Votes Dataset (KNARCV) [1963–2023]</summary>
<p>Roll call votes (<span dir="rtl" lang="ar">التصويت بنداء الأسماء</span>) cast on all laws passed by the Kuwait National Assembly, in which each legislator's or minister's position (agree, disagree, or abstain) is publicly recorded across the first and second deliberations. This dataset is at the legislator-vote level.</p>
<p>Publications:</p>
<ol>
  <li>Daniel L. Tavana and Erin York. 2025. "Legislative Cooptation in Authoritarian Regimes: Policy Cooperation in the Kuwait National Assembly." <em>British Journal of Political Science</em> 55. [<a href="https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007123424000371" target="_blank" rel="noopener">DOI</a>] [<a href="/assets/pdf/2025_bjps.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">PDF</a>]</li>
  <li>Mostafa El Sharkawy and Daniel L. Tavana. "Sacred Times, Sectarian Politics: Group Identity and Legislative Behavior in Authoritarian Regimes."</li>
</ol>
<p>Links:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Data</li>
  <li>Codebook</li>
</ul>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Kuwait National Assembly Laws Dataset [1963–2023]</summary>
<p>Laws published in the Official Gazette (<span dir="rtl" lang="ar">القوانين الصادرة بالجريدة الرسمية</span>) — all laws passed by the Kuwait National Assembly and ratified by the Amir, as printed in the state's legal journal, Kuwait Al-Youm. This dataset is at the law level.</p>
<p>Links:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Data</li>
  <li>Codebook</li>
</ul>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Kuwait National Assembly Draft Laws Dataset [1963–2023]</summary>
<p>Draft laws (<span dir="rtl" lang="ar">مشاريع بقانون</span>) — legislative proposals submitted by the government to the National Assembly for review and approval. This dataset is at the legislator-draft law level.</p>
<p>Links:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Data</li>
  <li>Codebook</li>
</ul>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Kuwait National Assembly Law Proposals Dataset [1963–2023]</summary>
<p>Law proposals (<span dir="rtl" lang="ar">اقتراح بقانون</span>) — legislative initiatives introduced by elected legislators rather than the government. This dataset is at the legislator-proposal level.</p>
<p>Links:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Data</li>
  <li>Codebook</li>
</ul>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Kuwait National Assembly Recommendations Dataset [1963–2023]</summary>
<p>Recommendations, or non-binding motions (<span dir="rtl" lang="ar">اقتراح برغبة</span>) — formal requests in which legislators ask the government to take a specific action. This dataset is at the legislator-recommendation level.</p>

</ol>

<ul>

<details>
<summary>Kuwait National Assembly Queries Dataset [1963–2023]</summary>
<p>Queries (<span dir="rtl" lang="ar">الأسئلة والردود</span>) — written questions submitted by legislators to obtain specific information from ministers. This dataset is at the legislator-query level.</p>

<p>Interpellations (<span dir="rtl" lang="ar">الاستجوابات</span>) — formal questioning of a minister or the Prime Minister that can culminate in a vote of no confidence. This dataset is at the legislator-interpellation level.</p>

<p>Amiri decrees (<span dir="rtl" lang="ar">المراسيم الأميرية</span>) — the primary legal instruments through which the Amir exercises executive power, countersigned by the Prime Minister and relevant ministers. This dataset is at the decree level.</p>
