### Iran

<details>
<summary>Islamic Consultative Assembly Election Results [2016]</summary>
<p>This dataset contains candidate-level results from the 2016 Islamic Consultative Assembly election. Results are disaggregated at the electoral district (N = 202) level.</p>
</details>

### Kuwait

<details>
<summary>Kuwait National Assembly Elections Dataset (KNAED) [1963–2016]</summary>
<p>The Kuwait National Assembly Elections Dataset (KNAED) is a panel dataset that includes every candidate who ran in any general election for the Kuwait National Assembly (KNA) from 1963 to 2016. The dataset includes a total of 4,514 observations, i.e., candidate-elections, for 2,615 unique individuals across 17 general elections. The dataset is based on a separate dataset, the Kuwait Politics Dataset, originally collected by Michael Herb.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Kuwait Tribal Primary Election Dataset (KTPED) [1963–2016]</summary>
<p>This dataset contains candidate-level results from every tribal primary held in Kuwait from 1963 to 2016.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Kuwait National Assembly Election Results [2016]</summary>
<p>This dataset contains candidate-level results from the 2016 Kuwait National Assembly election. Results are disaggregated at the polling station (N = 100) level.</p>
</details>

### Lebanon

<details>
<summary>Parliament of Lebanon Election Results [2018]</summary>
<p>This dataset contains candidate-level results from the 2018 Parliament of Lebanon election. Results are disaggregated at the polling station (N = 6,656) level.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Parliament of Lebanon Election Candidate Dataset [2018]</summary>
<p>This dataset contains candidate-level data from the 2018 Parliament of Lebanon election. Observations are included at the candidate (N = 576) level.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Parliament of Lebanon Election Voter Rolls [2018]</summary>
<p>This dataset contains anonymized records of registered voters from the 2018 Parliament of Lebanon election.</p>
</details>
